## Auto-update

TPV El Haido uses Tauri's native updater:

1. On launch, the app asks `haido.releases.mks2508.systems` whether a newer version exists.
2. If yes, the signed bundle is downloaded in the background.
3. A notification asks you to restart to apply the update.
4. Minisign signature verification guarantees the binary hasn't been tampered with during transit.

No manual reinstall needed after the first install.
